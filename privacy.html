Privacy

How DATALOG PTY LTD handles information associated with RecordLog.

Effective: April 2026 · Version 1.0

Your Data Stays Yours

DATALOG PTY LTD does not sell your personal data. We collect and process information needed to provide and support RecordLog’s features. You retain ownership of your photos and records.

1. Who We Are

RecordLog is a photo-documentation app published and operated by DATALOG PTY LTD in Victoria, Australia. For privacy questions or requests, email recordlogapp@gmail.com.

2. What We Collect

When RecordLog is used, the following categories of information may be collected:

  • Account information — such as an email address and display name used to create and manage an account
  • Photos and records — captured images and associated titles, notes, labels, project details, timestamps, and optional GPS coordinates
  • Device and diagnostic information — limited technical information used to operate, secure, diagnose, and improve the App
  • Support correspondence — information voluntarily included when contacting support

RecordLog does not currently collect or process payment card information.

3. How We Use Information

Information is processed to:

  • Create and authenticate accounts
  • Store and organise projects, photos, and related records
  • Generate and export structured PDF copies
  • Provide optional GPS and server timestamp metadata
  • Operate, secure, diagnose, and improve RecordLog
  • Respond to support and privacy requests

Personal information is not sold or used for third-party advertising.

4. GPS and Location Data

GPS is optional. When enabled for a project, RecordLog asks the device for a fresh location reading when a photo is captured and stores the available coordinates with that photo record. GPS accuracy varies with the device, signal, buildings, indoor conditions, and nearby obstructions. RecordLog does not intentionally collect GPS location in the background.

5. Data Storage and Security

RecordLog uses service providers, including Google Firebase, for functions such as authentication, database services, and file storage. We take reasonable steps to protect information, including secure authentication and encrypted transmission where supported. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

You are responsible for protecting account credentials and retaining copies of records you need.

6. Third-Party Services

Third-party providers may process information on our behalf to deliver hosting, authentication, storage, diagnostics, distribution, and related operational services. Their handling of information is also governed by their own terms and privacy practices.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably needed to provide RecordLog, maintain appropriate business and security records, respond to requests, and meet legal obligations. Account and data deletion requests will be handled in accordance with the available deletion process, operational requirements, and applicable law.

PDF copies saved or shared outside RecordLog remain wherever the user or recipient has stored them and are not removed by deleting an account.

8. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information associated with your account. Optional GPS can be disabled when creating a project. To make a privacy or data request, email recordlogapp@gmail.com.

9. Children’s Privacy

RecordLog is not intended for individuals under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information so that the matter can be reviewed.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as RecordLog, its operational processes, or legal requirements change. The effective date and version will be updated when a revised policy is published.

Questions about your privacy?

Email DATALOG PTY LTD at recordlogapp@gmail.com.
