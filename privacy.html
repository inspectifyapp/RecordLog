RecordLog Privacy Policy

Version: 2.0 · Effective date: 08/10/2026

RecordLog Privacy at a Glance

RecordLog uses account details, project information, photographs, device-generated capture times and optional location information to provide its photo documentation, organisation, cloud-record and PDF features. DATALOG PTY LTD does not sell personal information. This policy explains the information handled in connection with RecordLog, the parties that may process it and the choices available to you.

01.

Scope and Provider

This Privacy Policy applies to RecordLog’s iOS application and the supporting RecordLog website. Most collection described below occurs when you use the app. The current public website is static, does not provide a RecordLog account sign-in or project-upload function, and does not include analytics or advertising code. Website-hosting infrastructure may process technical request information, such as an IP address, browser information, requested URL and referring page, to deliver, monitor and secure the site under the provider’s applicable terms and policies. RecordLog is published and operated by DATALOG PTY LTD in Victoria, Australia. Privacy questions and requests may be sent to developer@datalog.com.au.

02.

Account Information

RecordLog uses Firebase Authentication for account creation and sign-in. Account information includes your email address, chosen display name and Firebase user ID. Firebase Authentication also handles verification and password-reset emails and may process technical information such as IP address and user agent. RecordLog does not place a readable password in its Firestore project records or local app preferences.

03.

Project, Photograph and Device Information

When you use RecordLog, the app may handle:

  • captured image files and related photograph records.
  • a SHA-256 hash calculated from the captured image file on the device.
  • GPS coordinates when project GPS is enabled and an appropriate location is available.
  • an optional photograph title of up to 20 characters and optional note of up to 50 characters.
  • photograph number and a capture date and time created from the device when the photograph is taken.
  • local app-file information, synchronisation status and a Firebase Storage download URL.
  • project name, optional project location, creation time, photograph count, project status, photograph order and project GPS setting.
  • technical information needed by Firebase and the device operating system to authenticate, deliver, secure and operate their services.

Project names, optional locations, titles, notes and photographs can contain personal information depending on what you enter or capture. RecordLog does not currently provide a separate category, area or label field and does not support importing photographs from the device photo library.

04.

Device-Generated Capture Times

RecordLog creates a capture date and time from the device when each photograph is taken. It is not a server-generated timestamp. It can be affected by the device’s date, time or time-zone settings and does not independently prove when a photograph was taken.

05.

Optional Location Data

While the RecordLog camera is active, the app may request and obtain device location. GPS is optional at the project level. RecordLog stores coordinates with a new photograph only when project GPS is enabled and an appropriate location is available. When project GPS is off, RecordLog does not persist coordinates with new photographs. The current app treats GPS as unavailable while offline. You can decline or revoke location permission in device settings and can create a project with GPS off. Location accuracy varies with the device, signal and surroundings.

06.

SHA-256 Photo Hashes

RecordLog calculates a SHA-256 hash from the captured image file on the device and may store that hash with the photograph record. The hash is a technical fingerprint that can help compare file contents. It does not certify the photograph, prove when or where it was taken, identify who captured it, or independently establish authenticity.

07.

How Information Is Used

DATALOG PTY LTD uses information handled through RecordLog to:

  • create, verify and authenticate accounts.
  • generate PDFs and provide system sharing options.
  • process deletion requests and provide support.
  • maintain, troubleshoot and secure RecordLog.
  • comply with applicable law and protect users, DATALOG PTY LTD and others.
  • save and display photograph details, capture times, optional coordinates and SHA-256 hashes.
  • create, synchronise, display and organise projects and photograph records.

DATALOG PTY LTD does not sell personal information or use RecordLog project content for advertising.

08.

Local Storage and Google Firebase

Saved photographs are kept in RecordLog’s app storage on the device and queued for cloud synchronisation. Generated PDFs are stored in the app’s documents area on the device. Pending synchronisation can resume when connectivity returns. RecordLog uses Firebase Authentication to process account and sign-in information, Cloud Firestore to store current project and photograph records, and Firebase Storage to store synchronised photograph image files in the app’s expected storage paths.

The current default Firestore database is configured in australia-southeast2 (Melbourne). Firebase states that Authentication processing occurs exclusively in the United States. The physical location of Firebase Storage and other provider processing may differ and is governed by the relevant provider arrangements. This policy does not promise that all RecordLog information remains in Australia. Google processes information needed to provide these services. This does not mean that every Google or Firebase service receives every type of RecordLog information.

09.

Access to RecordLog Information

  • Ordinary authenticated users: The current Firestore and Firebase Storage client security rules permit an authenticated user to access the implemented project and image paths only where the path user ID matches that user’s Firebase user ID. These client rules do not grant one ordinary user access to another ordinary user’s project paths.
  • DATALOG PTY LTD administrative access: Firebase management systems can provide authorised DATALOG PTY LTD accounts with administrative access separate from ordinary app-user access. DATALOG PTY LTD will use authorised administrative access only where reasonably necessary for support, security, maintenance, deletion handling or legal obligations and will apply appropriate account and organisational controls.
  • Google Firebase: Google processes account, database, storage and related technical information as the service provider for the Firebase functions used by RecordLog. Provider access and retention are governed by Google’s service controls, terms and policies.
  • Apple platform services: Apple provides App Store distribution and iOS platform functions. Apple does not receive RecordLog project records from Firebase merely because RecordLog runs on iOS. Apple or an Apple service may process information when an iOS function is used, subject to Apple’s terms and privacy practices.
10.

PDF Generation and External Sharing

RecordLog generates PDFs locally from project and photograph information available in the app. It then passes a selected PDF to the device’s system sharing options. RecordLog does not receive the destination or recipient selected after the file is handed to the system sharing interface. The receiving person, app or service controls the file after sharing and applies its own privacy practices.

11.

Disclosure

DATALOG PTY LTD may disclose information to Google Firebase as described above; to a receiving service when you choose to share a PDF or other file through the system interface; to professional advisers or service providers where reasonably necessary and subject to suitable obligations; or where required or authorised by law. RecordLog does not disclose project content to Apple merely because the app is distributed through the App Store.

12.

International Processing

DATALOG PTY LTD operates from Australia, but some service-provider processing occurs outside Australia. The current Firestore database is configured in Melbourne. Firebase Authentication processing occurs in the United States according to Firebase documentation. Firebase Storage and other provider processing may occur in locations permitted by the relevant provider arrangements. This policy does not promise that all RecordLog information remains in Australia.

13.

Retention and Deletion

Account and project information may be retained while your account is active. The current app does not apply an automatic fixed retention period to active RecordLog account, project or photograph records.

When you delete a photograph or project, RecordLog removes it from the visible project and performs or queues cleanup of the corresponding current Firestore record, expected Firebase Storage image and applicable local app file. If required cleanup cannot complete immediately, the app can retain the deletion request and retry when connectivity returns.

When you select "Delete Account", RecordLog requires confirmation and reauthentication for the signed-in email-and-password account. After successful reauthentication, RecordLog begins deleting the account and associated data handled by its current deletion process. It identifies current project and photograph records in its implemented Firebase paths, deletes those records and expected Firebase Storage images, and verifies the current project path and each expected image it attempted to delete before deleting the Firebase Authentication account. It then clears applicable local app records, photographs and generated project PDFs. If remote deletion or verification fails before Authentication deletion, the account is not removed and you can retry. If local cleanup fails after Authentication deletion, the account is already deleted and RecordLog signs out.

Deletion does not remove a PDF, photograph or other file previously exported, saved elsewhere or shared with another person or service. Provider backups, logs and other retained information may not be erased immediately. The implemented process does not search every possible Firebase location and may not identify historical orphaned records outside the current deletion paths.

14.

Security

RecordLog relies on device operating-system protections, authenticated Firebase services and user-specific client access rules for its current functionality. DATALOG PTY LTD takes reasonable steps appropriate to the service, but no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure. You are responsible for protecting your device and credentials and for keeping separate copies of important records.

15.

Analytics, Advertising and Payments

The current version of RecordLog does not include Firebase Analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, payment-processing or subscription SDKs. The current public website does not include analytics or advertising code. This policy will be updated if relevant app dependencies or website services change.

16.

Your Choices and Requests

You can choose whether project GPS is enabled, control location permission in device settings, edit available project and photograph details before locking, delete photographs or projects with the available controls, sign out, and request account deletion through the profile area. You may contact DATALOG PTY LTD to ask a privacy question or request assistance with access, correction or deletion. DATALOG PTY LTD will consider the request in light of the information it holds, the available technical controls and applicable law. No fixed response time is promised by this policy.

17.

Children

RecordLog account creation is intended for people aged 18 or older under the RecordLog Terms & Conditions. RecordLog is not directed to children.

18.

Changes to This Policy

DATALOG PTY LTD may update this policy when RecordLog, its providers or legal obligations change. The current version and effective date will be published in the app and on the RecordLog website. Where required by law, additional notice or consent will be provided.
