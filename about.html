About RecordLog

Document anything, keep a clear record.

Coming soon to the App Store

What RecordLog is for

RecordLog is a camera app with optional GPS tagging, capture timestamps, SHA-256 photo hashing and PDF export. Use it to document personal records, objects, vehicles, equipment, projects, plants, maintenance, work records, property or site conditions. Start a project, take your photos and generate a clear PDF ready to save or share.

What’s included

  • Clean PDF Export

    Generate a structured, organised PDF with your photos, labels and notes, ready to save or share.

  • Organised by Category

    Group photos by category, area or section. Drag to reorder and keep each project easy to review.

  • GPS Tagging

    Optional. RecordLog obtains device coordinates with each photo when GPS is enabled for the project. Accuracy varies with the device and surroundings.

  • Capture Timestamps

    Records the photo capture date and time so it remains available while cloud sync is pending. Capture timestamps are independent of GPS availability.

  • Locked Records

    Once generated, your project is locked from further edits in the app and stored in Firebase.

  • SHA-256 Hashing

    Each photo receives a unique SHA-256 hash to help identify changes to the file.

Who uses RecordLog

RecordLog is for people and businesses that need clear, dated photo records with useful context. Examples include personal records, objects, vehicles, equipment, projects, plants, maintenance, work records, property or site conditions, insurance or warranty documentation, and general household or business record-keeping.

How it works

1. Create a project

Give the record a clear name, add an optional location and choose whether GPS is on.

2. Take photos in RecordLog

Use the RecordLog camera. Capture time is recorded with each photo, with GPS added when enabled and available.

3. Add notes and labels

Add a title, label and optional note (max 50 chars).

4. Review and reorder

Review photos in the gallery, drag to reorder within each group, and correct details before generating the record.

5. Generate and export

Generate a clean, organised PDF. The project is then locked from further edits and ready to save or share.
