06.

Timestamps, GPS and SHA-256 Hashes

RecordLog creates a capture date and time from the device when each photograph is taken. It is not a server-generated timestamp. Device settings or errors can affect the recorded time. While the RecordLog camera is active, the app may request and obtain device location. GPS is optional at the project level. RecordLog stores coordinates with a new photograph only when project GPS is enabled and an appropriate location is available. The current app treats GPS as unavailable while offline. Coordinates and accuracy depend on the device, signal and surroundings and may not represent an exact position. RecordLog calculates a SHA-256 hash from the captured image file on the device. A matching or changed hash can assist with comparing file contents, but the hash does not certify the photograph, prove when or where it was taken, identify who captured it, or independently establish authenticity.

You are responsible for checking the project before generating its PDF and for storing any exported PDF safely.
