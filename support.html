Getting Started

Account setup

RecordLog is not publicly released yet. At launch, new users will create an account with their name, email address, and password, verify their email, and then sign in.

What RecordLog documents

RecordLog is designed for ordinary photo documentation of property, vehicles, equipment, belongings, site conditions, and general personal or business records. It does not inspect, assess, certify, verify, or formally validate what is captured.

Creating a Project

Start a new project

A project brings related photos and details together. Give it a clear name and add an optional location or address.

A descriptive name makes the record easier to find later.

Taking Photos

Capture photos in RecordLog

RecordLog uses its in-app camera so each captured photo can be recorded with consistent supporting information. Importing existing gallery photos is not part of the planned workflow.

Capture on location

If optional GPS is enabled, capture photos while present at the asset so the device can obtain the most relevant available coordinates.

Use clear lighting

Well-lit photos show detail more clearly in the final PDF copy.

Reviewing and Organising Photos

Review and label each photo

Add an optional title, area label, and notes of up to 300 characters to provide useful context.

Organise the record

Photos can be grouped by area and reordered before the record is generated.

Use consistent labels

Clear, consistent area names and short notes make the resulting PDF easier to read.

Generating and Sharing Reports

Generate a structured PDF

RecordLog compiles captured photos, labels, and notes into a structured, watermarked PDF copy that can be saved or shared.

Stored record and PDF copy

The stored RecordLog record is the underlying record. The exported PDF is a structured, shareable copy.

GPS and Timestamps

Optional GPS

GPS can be enabled for a project. When enabled, RecordLog obtains a fresh location reading from the device when each photo is captured.

GPS accuracy

Accuracy varies with signal strength, buildings, indoor conditions, nearby obstructions, and the device. Coordinates may remain similar for photos taken close together.

Camera preview and saved location

The camera preview updates periodically as a reassurance display. The location stored for a photo is obtained again at capture, so it may differ slightly from the preview.

Server timestamps

Server timestamps require connectivity. Without a connection, a server timestamp cannot be confirmed at capture.

Locked Records

Review before generating

Generating the record locks it against further editing. Review photos, labels, notes, and ordering before generation. Create a new project when an updated record is needed.

Account, Privacy and Data

Your information

RecordLog uses account information, captured photos, details, and optional metadata to provide its documentation features. See the Privacy Policy for the current public explanation.

Deletion and retention

Account and data requests will be handled in accordance with the Privacy Policy, operational requirements, and applicable law. Contact support for assistance.

Troubleshooting

Why can’t I add gallery photos?

RecordLog’s planned workflow uses its own camera so supporting information can be captured consistently when the photo is taken. Existing gallery photos are therefore not supported.
